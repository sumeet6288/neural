## 🔷 LAYER 1 — SMART CONTRACTS (CORE LOGIC)

Create separate, modular smart contracts:

### 1. Token Contract (ERC20)

* Mintable + burnable
* Controlled emission (not unlimited)
* Transaction fees (buy/sell tax)
* Fee distribution:

  * Treasury
  * Liquidity
  * Rewards
* Anti-whale limits (optional)

---

### 2. Treasury Contract

* Holds stable assets (USDT / USDC)
* Responsible for:

  * Buybacks
  * Liquidity support
  * Reward funding
* Only callable by authorized contracts or DAO
* Fully transparent

---

### 3. Staking Contract

Support:

#### A. Bond Staking

* Lock durations: 45 / 90 / 180 / 360 days
* Higher ROI

#### B. Flexible Staking

* No lock
* Lower ROI

Must include:

* Reward tracking
* Auto-compounding logic
* Time-based calculations

---

### 4. Referral & Ranking Contract

* Multi-level referral tracking
* Direct reward: 10%
* Rank system (15 levels)
* Rank criteria:

  * Stake amount
  * Team volume
  * Referral count
* ROI-on-ROI bonus logic

---

### 5. DAO Governance Contract

* Proposal system
* Voting system
* Execution system
* Voting power = stake + duration + rank
* Controls:

  * Emission rates
  * Fees
  * Treasury allocation
  * Risk parameters

---

### 6. Borrowing & Lending Contract

* Collateral-based loans
* Loan-to-Value (LTV) logic
* Interest calculation
* Liquidation engine
* Treasury integration

---

### 7. Stablecoin Contract

* Minted against collateral
* Backed by treasury reserves
* Used internally for borrowing/lending

## 🔷 LAYER 2 — AI SIMULATION ENGINE (VERY IMPORTANT)

Implement 5 core modules (rule-based, not real AI):

### NEE (Emission Engine)

* Controls minting and burning
* Adjusts supply dynamically

---

### ALS (Liquidity Stabilizer)

* Maintains price corridor
* Executes:

  * Buybacks when price drops
  * Controlled selling when price spikes

---

### ARP (Auto Reinvest Protocol)

* Collects all protocol fees
* Sends funds to treasury
* Reinvests into liquidity

---

### SIG (Supply Integrity Guard)

* Validates mint requests
* Prevents oversupply
* Ensures treasury backing

---

### ALP (Adaptive Logic Predictor)

* Monitors system health
* Adjusts:

  * emission rates
  * reward rates
* Ensures long-term sustainability
